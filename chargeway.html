01 — OVERVIEW

Making EV charging
easier across Jordan.

ChargeWay is a mobile application designed to help electric vehicle drivers find suitable charging stations, check charger compatibility, compare charging prices, plan their routes, and discover useful stops along the way.

ROLE UI/UX Designer
TOOLS Figma · UI/UX · Prototyping
PLATFORM Mobile App
02 — THE PROBLEM

EV charging shouldn't feel complicated.

Finding a suitable charging station can be difficult, especially when drivers need to know whether a charger is compatible with their vehicle, how much charging costs, and how long it will take to reach the station.

Drivers also need useful information around the station, such as nearby restaurants, cafes, fuel stations, and available facilities.

03 — THE SOLUTION

One place to plan the entire journey.

ChargeWay brings charging discovery, vehicle compatibility, pricing, navigation, and trip planning into one mobile experience designed specifically for drivers across Jordan.

The experience helps users make faster decisions before and during their journey.
